# 🚗 Austo Automobile Customer & Sales Analysis

An end-to-end Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project on customer purchasing behaviour for Austo Automobile. This project analyzes demographic, financial, and behavioural factors influencing automobile purchases using Python, providing actionable business insights to improve marketing strategies and customer targeting.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Austo Automobile wanted to evaluate the effectiveness of its current marketing strategy by understanding customer purchasing patterns across different vehicle categories (SUV, Sedan, and Hatchback).

This project performs data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, and business-focused visualizations to identify the characteristics of customers most likely to purchase each automobile segment.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Perform comprehensive data cleaning and preprocessing
- Explore customer demographics and purchasing behaviour
- Identify factors influencing automobile preferences
- Answer key business questions using data
- Generate actionable recommendations for marketing teams

---

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains **1,581 customer records** with **14 variables**, including:

- Age
- Gender
- Profession
- Marital Status
- Education
- Number of Dependents
- Personal Loan
- House Loan
- Partner Working Status
- Salary
- Partner Salary
- Total Salary
- Automobile Price
- Vehicle Make (SUV, Sedan, Hatchback)

## 💡 Key Business Insights

- Sedan is the most preferred automobile segment among customers.
- Female customers exhibit a stronger preference for SUVs than males.
- Salaried professionals are the largest customer segment.
- Higher-income customers are more likely to purchase SUVs.
- Hatchbacks are preferred by younger and budget-conscious customers.
- Married customers predominantly purchase Sedans.
- Customers with higher combined household income tend to purchase premium vehicles.
- Personal loan holders show a greater inclination toward Sedan purchases.
